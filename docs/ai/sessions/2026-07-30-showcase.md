# AI session: public reviewer showcase

- Date: 2026-07-30
- Goal: turn the verified systems evidence into an accessible public reviewer
  experience without deploying a controller, ingestion API, database, or
  credential.
- Human direction: create a new public repository, deploy it at
  `demo.marquesco.com`, and make the commit history demonstrate strong AI
  co-working.
- AI tool: OpenAI Codex.

## Approach and boundary

Codex proposed a dependency-free static site rather than placing the FastAPI PDF
boundary or local simulator on the public internet. The site explains
architecture, replays the checked-in 15-step integration evidence, independently
decodes the normative binary frame, and makes human direction versus AI
contribution explicit.

The browser trace is labeled recorded evidence. It does not claim to execute the
C++ controller. The browser decoder is explanatory and makes no trust or
authentication decision.

## Human and AI roles

- Human accepted a new clean public repository and isolated Google Cloud
  project after previously keeping the implementation local.
- Human required the public commit history itself to demonstrate organized AI
  co-working.
- Codex preserved the seven focused implementation commits through a subtree
  split, confirmed the private parent is not an ancestor, and designed the
  public review surface.
- Codex kept external runtime and document-processing attack surfaces out of
  the deployment.
